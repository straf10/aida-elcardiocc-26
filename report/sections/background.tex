\section{Background}
\label{sec:background}

Automatic ICD coding from clinical text has been addressed with CNN/RNN architectures~\cite{grbert_doc_pooling}, BERT-based models~\cite{devlin2019bert,lee2020biobert}, and hybrid retrieval-classification pipelines~\cite{dong2022automated}. For Greek clinical NLP, Greek BERT~\cite{koutsikakis2020greekbert} provides a domain-relevant starting point, while XLM-RoBERTa~\cite{conneau2020unsupervised} handles Greek--Latin code-switching in clinical abbreviations. Label imbalance is the central challenge in multi-label coding: Asymmetric Loss (ASL)~\cite{ridnik2021asl} addresses this by suppressing easy negatives more aggressively than positives. Ensemble methods~\cite{dietterich2000ensemble} and VNS-based search~\cite{mladenovic1997variable} over non-differentiable objectives complement neural approaches for rare-label coverage.
