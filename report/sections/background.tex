\section{Background}
\label{sec:background}

\subsection{Transformer Language Models for Clinical NLP}

BERT~\cite{devlin2019bert} pre-trains a deep bidirectional Transformer~\cite{vaswani2017attention}
using masked language modelling, producing contextual representations that transfer
effectively to downstream tasks. Domain-specific variants such as BioBERT~\cite{lee2020biobert}
consistently outperform general-purpose models on medical benchmarks. For Greek clinical
text we use Greek BERT~\cite{koutsikakis2020greekbert} (35K-token vocabulary, pre-trained on Wikipedia,
European Parliament proceedings, and web text).
XLM-RoBERTa~\cite{conneau2020unsupervised} extends RoBERTa~\cite{liu2019roberta} to
100 languages with a 250K multilingual SentencePiece vocabulary, naturally handling the
Greek--Latin code-switching present in clinical abbreviations (PCI, TAVI, CABG).

\subsection{Multi-Label Classification and Loss Functions}

Each discharge summary is assigned any subset of 115 ICD-10 codes. A sigmoid-activated
linear head produces per-label probabilities; a per-label threshold determines the final
binary prediction. Label imbalance is the central challenge: standard BCE is dominated
by easy negatives.
\textbf{Asymmetric Loss (ASL)}~\cite{ridnik2021asl} applies a stronger focal
factor to negatives than to positives, with a probability-shift margin $m$ that
suppresses confidently-correct negatives before computing the log.
\textbf{ZLPR} is a ranking-style objective that directly optimises the margin between
positive and negative label logits without requiring calibrated probabilities.

\subsection{Retrieval and Ensemble Methods}

BM25 and TF-IDF provide sparse retrieval; dense retrieval
encodes both query and candidates into a shared embedding space.
\textbf{RRF}~\cite{cormack2009reciprocal} merges ranked lists score-free:
$\text{RRF}(d)=\sum_i w_i/(k+\text{rank}_i(d))$, $k=60$.
\textbf{VNS}~\cite{mladenovic1997variable} widens the search neighbourhood when no
improvement is found, helping avoid local optima. It works well for
non-differentiable objectives such as micro-F1.
