\section{Task and Dataset}
\label{sec:task}

\subsection{Task Definition}

ElCardioCC 2026 is a multi-label ICD-10 coding shared task for Greek cardiology
discharge summaries (BioASQ/CLEF 2026), with 115 target codes. The primary metric is
\textbf{relaxed micro-F1}: predictions from the same clinical cluster as the gold label
are not fully penalised, reflecting clinical equivalence among related codes.

\subsection{Dataset}

The dataset consists of 2,500 anonymised discharge summaries split into training (2,000),
validation (250), and test (250) sets, with a separate blind test set held by the
organisers. Documents are moderately long: mean 280 tokens ($\approx$2,000 characters),
maximum 1,218 tokens. Only 2.4\% of documents exceed the 512-token BERT limit.

\textbf{Label distribution.} The 115 codes are highly skewed. The five most frequent
(Z95, I25, I21, I48, E11) account for $\sim$41\% of all annotations. At the other
extreme, 30 codes appear fewer than 10 times and 4 codes appear only once in the entire
dataset (Table~\ref{tab:label_stats}). This long-tailed distribution severely challenges
standard classifiers and requires explicit strategies for rare-label coverage.

\subsection{Preprocessing and Data Augmentation}

\textbf{Text cleaning.} Shared across all splits: whitespace collapse, removal of
non-essential special characters (preserving hyphens, commas, slashes, parentheses).
Greek BERT additionally receives lowercasing and accent stripping; XLM-RoBERTa
receives whitespace-only normalisation to preserve abbreviation signals (``PCI'',
``TAVI''). ICD-10 annotations are encoded as 115-dimensional binary multilabel vectors.

\textbf{Stratified splitting.} We apply a custom two-stage Multilabel Stratified Shuffle
Split~\cite{sechidis2011stratification} producing an 80/10/10 train/val/test partition.
Singleton labels (count$=$1) are forced to train; labels with 2--4 instances are
guaranteed at least one occurrence in both train and test; labels with $\geq$5
instances are guaranteed at least one in each split. A final patch pass ensures every
label seen in val or test is also present in train. Of the 2,500 samples, 1,453 include
mention-level annotations enabling NER+EL training.

\textbf{Synonym augmentation} (XLM-RoBERTa base only). Dictionary terms are swapped
with clinical synonyms at $p=0.35$. Multipliers are frequency-adaptive:
$\times$4 (30--99 instances), $\times$3 (100--199), $\times$2 (200--299); labels
outside this range are left unaugmented.
